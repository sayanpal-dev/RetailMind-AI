RetailMind Customer Support Policy

Returns:
Products may be returned within 7 calendar days of delivery.

Return Conditions:
The product must be unused and returned with the original packaging,
invoice, tags and accessories.

Damaged Products:
Customers must report damaged or defective products within 48 hours
of delivery.

Refunds:
Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method within
5 to 7 business days after the returned product passes inspection.

Exchanges:
Size exchanges depend on stock availability.

Cancellation:
Orders may be cancelled before shipment. An order cannot be cancelled
after shipment.

Delivery:
Standard delivery normally takes 3 to 7 business days.

Unsupported Questions:
If the available product information or policy does not contain the
answer, the assistant must say that the information is unavailable
and recommend contacting a human support representative.
